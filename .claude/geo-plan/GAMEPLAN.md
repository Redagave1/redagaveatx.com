# Red Agave: 90-day game plan for AI and search visibility

Written 2026-11-18 from the Pixis Visibility audit (10 questions, four AI assistants) and a check of the live site.
Goal: be named when people ask AI assistants about Austin landscaping, and move the audit score from 31 toward 50.

Where we start: named on 2 of 10 questions. Cited in 15 of 78 answers that list sources. Link authority 2 of 100.

## What "citations" means here, and how each kind is built

1. **Listings.** Your name, address, phone, hours, and services, written the same way on every directory. AI assistants read these to decide who you are. Built by claiming each profile and pasting in one master description.
2. **Links and mentions from other websites.** A supplier, a magazine, a "best of" list, or an architect naming you and linking to you. This is what the audit scores 2 of 100. Built by asking: one request at a time, each with a reason to say yes (a finished project, a product you install, a membership).
3. **AI assistants quoting your own pages.** They quote pages that answer the question in the first two sentences, give numbers and a table, show a real job, and link to outside sources. Built by writing those pages.
