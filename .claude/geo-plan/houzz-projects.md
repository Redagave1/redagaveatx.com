## Descriptions

**Floating Triangle Planter and Terraced Steel, Southeast Austin**

Terraced black steel beds with a custom light rim around the outside, and a welded steel triangle planter that floats on one post above them. The planter has an angle iron rim and an angle iron light rim. Planted with a variegated century agave, silver ponyfoot, silver santolina, maiden grass, and artichoke agave in basalt gravel. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Round Water Feature and Two Steel Fire Pits, Southeast Austin**

A round raised water feature built like a small pool: a concrete shell, a plaster interior finished black, and a sand pool filter. Water comes out of a drilled boulder and runs down a steel channel to the pool. A floating steel fire pit curves along the front, and a second fire pit is suspended between two boulders. Concrete pads with gravel joints, a steel planter welded on site, and lit steel edging. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Patio Courtyard With Boulder Fountains and a Floating Fire Pit, Pflugerville**

An existing patio expanded with custom poured concrete pads, each edge framed in steel with lighting in the joints. A square steel fire pit set on the diagonal floats above the planting bed, and two boulders are drilled and plumbed to run water. A steel-framed planter holds an Anacacho orchid tree, with river fern, creeping Jenny, and horsetail reed around the boulders. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Cantilevered Steel Planters and Steel Retaining Wall, Tarrytown**

Two cantilevered steel planters frame the front entry, stacked and offset, on welded steel legs above gravel and Mexican beach pebbles. Angle iron runs the top rim and holds the lighting. The steel is darkened and sealed. Planted with Agave weberi, artichoke agave, and blue spruce sedum among boulders, with a steel retaining wall in the back yard. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Steel Terraces, Fire Features and Deck on a Slope, Circle C**

A sloped back yard cut into five levels: three steel terraces stepping down through gravel and boulders, and two deck levels off the house. Quarter-inch steel edges every level. Two fire pits anchor the upper terrace, beside cantilevered steel planters filled with crushed turquoise glass and lit from below. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Deck Over Flagstone, Steel Pergola Panels and Boulder Fire Pit, Circle C**

A new yard built over the old one. Composite decking covers the old flagstone patio in one plane, and the original pergola was reframed and capped with custom-cut steel triangles that throw shadows across the deck. A boulder fire pit is set into the steps, concrete pavers run through synthetic turf, and cantilevered steel planters with lighting trace the edge. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Black Cantera Wall, Marbled Concrete and Floating Fire Pit, Austin**

A block wall rebuilt and faced with black cantera stone set on a welded steel ledge, with lighting under the steel. Large concrete slabs poured to look like marble, each edged in quarter-inch welded steel. A concrete fire pit on hidden legs floats above the patio with light under it. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Large Pavers in Turf, Floating Steel Fire Pits and Native Planting, Cedar Creek**

Large concrete pavers poured with synthetic turf joints beside a new pool. The yard is terraced with quarter-inch welded steel. Two custom floating steel fire pits: one long with amber fire glass, and one square with aqua glass at the pool edge. Bermuda sod and a native plant palette. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Terraced Turf Yard With Drilled Boulder Fire Pit and Lighting, Austin**

A bare lot terraced into two connected levels, with synthetic turf across both. Black boulders were set and drilled on site: one is a floating bench, and one is a fire pit with the gas burner run through the stone. Lighting is buried through the landscape. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Steel-Framed Screened Room With Cedar Skylights, South Austin**

A welded steel frame on a concrete slab at the back doors, finished as a screened room. Square steel tube finished black, a ribbed metal roof, a cedar plank ceiling, two skylights with cedar-lined wells, a ceiling fan, and black-framed screens on three sides. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.

**Steel Edging Cut Into Boulders, East Austin**

Quarter-inch steel edging that runs through the boulders in a front yard. A slot is cut into each stone with an angle grinder and the steel is seated inside it, so the edge and the stone read as one piece. Designed and built by Red Agave Landscape.
