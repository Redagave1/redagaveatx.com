## What Should You Compare?

Seven things. Ask every landscaper on your list the same questions.

- **Who draws the plan?**
- **Who builds it?**
- **Can they show a plan next to the finished yard?**
- **Do they make their own steel?**
- **What do they build that no one else does?**
- **How much have they built, and for how long?**
- **What is on paper before you sign?**
