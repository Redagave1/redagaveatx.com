**Red Agave Landscape designs and builds custom fire pits in Austin, TX, and each one starts as a mark on the owner's plan.** In this Southeast Austin backyard, the plan put two fire pits around a round water feature. Built, one is a curved steel fire pit that follows the pool wall, and the other is a steel pan held between two boulders. Here is the drawing, and the finished yard at dusk.

## Where Does a Fire Pit Go on the Plan?

Beside something. A fire pit dropped in the middle of a lawn has nothing to do with the rest of the yard. On this plan, both fires sit against boulders, a few steps from the water.

The drawing sets five things:

- **The patio:** concrete pads along the back of the house, with a joint between each pad
- **The water:** a round pool at the corner where the pads meet the gravel, fed by a channel that runs from a boulder
- **The fire:** two fire pits, each drawn beside boulders
- **The gravel:** a dark gravel court off the patio, with a dashed line around its edge
- **The planting:** a bed with boulders at the end of the patio, and lawn beyond

We plan gas, electrical, and drainage first, so finished work is never torn up for a line. A fire pit is decided on the drawing for that reason.

## What Changed Between the Drawing and the Fire?

The shapes. On the plan, both fire pits are plain rectangles. Built, each one has a shape of its own.

[[fig images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/21-water-feature-curved-fire-pit-dusk-800.webp | Curved steel fire pit burning at the foot of a round raised water feature at dusk, with a steel planter behind, in Southeast Austin ;; images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/22-water-feature-channel-fire-top-view-800.webp | Steel channel pouring water into a round black water feature above a curved steel fire pit filled with dark fire glass in Southeast Austin || The front fire pit became a curve that follows the pool wall. A steel channel carries water from the boulder to the pool.]]

- **The front fire pit became a curve.** It is a steel fire pit bent to follow the round wall of the pool. It has a custom burner, and it is set off the concrete, so it reads as floating.
- **The second fire pit became a pan.** It is a square steel pan with a ring burner, suspended between two boulders behind the pool.
- **A planter was added.** A square steel planter on a tapered base stands behind the pool and holds a tree. It is not on this drawing.

The layout held. The pads, the round pool, the channel from the boulder, and the dark gravel court are all where the plan drew them.

## Why Put Fire Next to Water?

Because each one makes the other better after dark. The front fire pit sits at the foot of the pool wall, so the flames light the wall. The inside of the pool is finished black, and black water works like a dark mirror: it reflects the fire in front of it and the sky above it.

[[fig images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/24-water-feature-fire-pits-overview-1200.webp | Round water feature with a curved steel fire pit in front, a second fire pit between boulders behind, a steel planter, and lit steel edging at dusk in Southeast Austin || Both fires from the patio. The second fire pit burns between its two boulders, behind the pool.]]

The sound is part of it too. Water comes out of a hole drilled in a boulder, runs down the steel channel, and falls to the surface.

How the pool itself was built, with a concrete shell, a plaster interior, and a sand pool filter, is in our post on [the water feature and fire pits in Southeast Austin](water-feature-fire-pits-southeast-austin-tx).

## What Holds the Second Fire Pit?

Two boulders. The square steel pan hangs between them, so the fire sits above the gravel with stone on both sides.

[[fig images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/23-boulder-fire-pit-lit-edging-800.webp | Square steel fire pit suspended between two boulders in a dark gravel court with lit steel edging, beside a round water feature in Southeast Austin ;; images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/25-water-feature-fire-pit-house-800.webp | Curved steel fire pit burning in front of a round water feature at dusk with a steel planter and a tree behind in Southeast Austin ;; images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/26-steel-planter-base-river-rock-800.webp | Tapered steel base of a square planter with a slot packed with river rock and lit from inside in Southeast Austin || The boulder fire pit in its gravel court, the curved fire pit from the patio, and the lit base of the steel planter.]]

The court around it is the dark gravel area from the plan. Steel edging holds the gravel, and a light strip runs along the steel, so the outline of the court shows at night.

The planter is lit as well. Its tapered base has a slot packed with river rock, with a light behind the rock.

Gas. The curved fire pit has a custom burner and burns over dark fire glass. The square pan has a ring burner.
