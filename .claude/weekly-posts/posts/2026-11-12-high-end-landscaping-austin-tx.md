## What Makes Landscaping High-End?

Not the price of any one feature. A yard reads as high-end when everything in it was decided together: the levels, the materials, the fire, the water, and the light. Six things do that work.

- **A plan drawn by the owner**
- **Steel cut and welded on site**
- **Real materials, chosen with samples in hand**
- **Fire and water built in**
- **Light built into the structure**
- **One crew, from the first day to the last**

## Why Does It Start With a Plan?

Because a luxury yard is one design. Every Red Agave project starts with a free consultation, and the owner, Jensen Mehrens, draws the plan. It sets the layout, the sizes, and the materials before anything is ordered.

In Circle C, that plan turned a bare slope into two deck levels, a gravel fire court, and steel terraces. You can see the drawing next to the finished yard in our post on [landscape design build](landscape-design-build-austin-tx), and more drawings on our [design plans](../designs) page.

## What Does Custom Steel Add?

Shapes you cannot buy. We cut steel plate and weld it on site, so the steel fits the yard and not the other way around.

## Which Materials Belong in a Luxury Yard?

Ones that are what they look like. In East Austin the deck is teak, and it runs flush from a glass-walled screened porch to an outdoor kitchen, with no step and no transition strip. The pool beside it has natural stone coping. The porch ceiling is stained tongue-and-groove cedar.

We bring material and color samples to you, and you approve every selection before we order. See our [custom decks](../services/custom-decks-austin-tx) and [pools](../services/pools-austin-tx) pages.

## Why Build Fire and Water In?

Because they give a yard a center. In Southeast Austin we built a round water feature the way a pool is built, with a concrete shell, a plaster interior, and a sand pool filter. Water runs from a drilled boulder down a steel channel. Two custom fire pits burn beside it.

[[fig images/projects/se-austin-fire-water/24-water-feature-fire-pits-overview-1200.webp | Round water feature with a curved steel fire pit in front, a second fire pit between boulders, a steel planter, and lit steel edging at dusk in Southeast Austin || Southeast Austin: water, two fires, and a steel planter, with light along every steel edge.]]

Our post on [custom fire pits](custom-fire-pits-austin-tx) shows the plan for this yard next to the finished fires.

## How Does Lighting Change a Yard?

It decides what the yard is after dark. We build light into the structure: under pergola beams, under the rims of steel planters, and along steel and deck edges. The source stays out of sight, and the light lands where it is used.

[[fig images/projects/hopeland/IMG_9683.webp | Black dome pendant lights under pergola beams over a dining table at dusk, with a lit steel planter and a slatted cabana beyond, in Circle C, Austin || Circle C: pendants under the pergola beams, and a steel planter lit from under its rim.]]

See our post on [landscape lighting installation](landscape-lighting-installation-austin-tx).
