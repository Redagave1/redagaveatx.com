## How Much Does Xeriscaping or Synthetic Turf Cost in Austin?

Xeriscaping is priced by the project. Our [xeriscaping cost guide](xeriscaping-cost-austin-tx) puts most Austin projects between $5,000 and $45,000, in three sizes:

- **Under 500 square feet:** $5,000 to $12,000. A front yard strip, a side yard, or a courtyard
- **500 to 1,500 square feet:** $12,000 to $28,000. A full front yard
- **Over 1,500 square feet:** $28,000 to $45,000 or more. A whole yard

Synthetic turf is priced by the square foot. Our [synthetic turf cost guide](synthetic-turf-cost-austin-tx) puts it at $7 to $12 per square foot fully installed. That is $3,500 to $6,000 for 500 square feet, and $7,000 to $12,000 for 1,000. ## How Much Does a Fence or a Deck Cost in Austin?

A fence is priced by the linear foot. Our [fence cost guide](fence-cost-austin-tx) gives $15 to $65 per linear foot fully installed, with chain link at the low end and steel and wrought iron at the top. Gates are priced separately, and the guide budgets $200 to $600 for a walk-through gate.

A deck is priced by the square foot, and the wood sets the rate. Our [deck cost guide](deck-cost-austin-tx) gives cedar at $30 to $50 per square foot installed and IPE hardwood at $55 to $80. ## How Are Patios, Walls, Pools, and Outdoor Kitchens Priced?

Each has its own guide, because the price turns on choices made early.

- **Retaining wall:** priced by the square foot of wall face, which is height times length. A wall over 4 feet costs more per square foot, because it needs an engineer and a permit. See the [retaining wall cost guide](retaining-wall-cost-austin-tx)

## What Moves a Landscaping Price in Austin?

The ground and the City's rules, more than the size of the yard. Our guides give six reasons.

- **Rock and soil:** the west side of Austin sits on shallow limestone or [caliche](caliche-soil-preparation-austin-tx), and the central and east side on deep, dark clay. Our fence guide adds $500 to $2,000 to a project where posts are set in rocky ground. Our drainage and water feature guides add $500 to $3,000 where limestone has to be cut
- **Slope:** a sloped fence line adds 10 to 25 percent. An elevated deck costs 30 to 50 percent more than one at ground level. Synthetic turf on a slope adds 50 to 60 percent
- **Access:** equipment needs a clear path to the backyard, at least 36 inches wide for a wheelbarrow and 6 to 8 feet for a mini excavator. Where a machine cannot get in, the digging is done by hand, and that costs more
- **Trees:** inside Austin city limits, the City Arborist program protects trees with a diameter of 19 inches or more, so the layout works around them. A fence line routed around a protected root zone takes more material and labor
- **Permits:** the City of Austin's list of [work exempt from building permits](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/work-exempt-building-permits) stops at fences 7 feet high, retaining walls 4 feet tall measured from the bottom of the footing, and detached decks of 200 square feet that sit no more than 30 inches above grade. Work past those limits needs a permit, and a wall over 4 feet needs an engineer
- **Material:** in our deck guide, the most expensive decking costs roughly three times the least expensive

## FAQ: Landscaping Cost in Austin

### How much does landscaping cost in Austin, TX?
The total is the sum of what is built. Our cost guides put synthetic turf at $7 to $12 per square foot installed, a fence at $15 to $65 per linear foot, drainage at $2,000 to $10,000 for most residential projects, and most xeriscape projects at $5,000 to $45,000.

### Why do two quotes for the same yard differ?
Most often because they do not cover the same work. Our [backyard renovation guide](backyard-renovation-guide-austin-tx) says the gap between the cheapest bid and a mid-range bid is almost always in base preparation, material quality, plant sizes, and warranty. An itemized quote lets you compare line by line.

### Does Red Agave charge for a landscape design?
There is no separate charge. The consultation is free, and the CAD plan is included with the project.

### Can a landscape be built in phases?
Yes, when the whole yard is planned first. Our [outdoor living guide](outdoor-living-space-guide-austin-tx) puts the patio, drainage, grading, and utility lines in the first phase, then the main feature, then shade and lighting, then the last of the planting.

### Do I need a permit for landscaping in Austin?
Not for every job. The City of Austin's list of exempt work includes fences not over 7 feet high and retaining walls not over 4 feet, measured from the bottom of the footing. A homeowners association can require its own approval first. See our approval guides for [Circle C](circle-c-hoa-landscape-approval-austin-tx), [Steiner Ranch](steiner-ranch-hoa-landscape-approval-austin-tx), and [Mueller](mueller-hoa-landscape-approval-austin-tx).
