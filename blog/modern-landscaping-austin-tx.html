Modern Landscaping in Austin, TX: What a Modern Yard Is Built From

November 20, 2026

Modern landscaping in Austin, TX is built from four things: steel, large-format concrete, light with no visible source, and plants chosen for their shape. Red Agave Landscape has designed and built modern yards in Austin since 2009, and was one of the first companies in the city to work in welded steel. This post shows each of the four parts on a yard we built, from Tarrytown to Cedar Creek.

What Makes a Landscape Modern?

A short list of materials, straight lines, and nothing added for decoration. Most of our yards use three or four materials together, and each one does a job:

  • Steel draws the line
  • Concrete makes the floor
  • Light shows the structure after dark
  • Plants give it shape that lasts all year

Our list of 20 modern backyard ideas covers single features. This post covers the four parts they are made from.

Why Does Steel Draw the Line?

Because it is thin, and it can be cut to any line. A steel wall takes almost no room. We use quarter-inch steel, and we cut and weld it on site.

Three of our front and back yards are built on it:

  • Southeast Austin: black steel beds step toward the front door, and a welded steel triangle planter floats above them on one post. The steel is sealed with a black penetrating sealer.
  • Tarrytown: two cantilevered steel planters frame the front entry, stacked and offset, on welded steel legs above gravel and Mexican beach pebbles. The steel is darkened and sealed.
  • Overlook at Lewis Mountain, Southwest Austin: a bare slope cut into three steel terraces, with quarter-inch steel on every edge, 260 linear feet in all.

See our custom steel planters, our steel retaining walls, and the Southeast Austin and Tarrytown project pages.

What Does Large-Format Concrete Do?

It gives the yard a floor made of a few big pieces. We often pour a patio as separate pads, with turf, gravel, or river rock in the joints. Pads read as lighter than one large slab, and rain soaks in between them.

  • Cedar Creek: large pavers poured beside a pool, with artificial turf in every joint
  • Austin: large slabs poured to look like marble, each edged in 1/4-inch welded steel
  • Pearson Ranch, Northwest Austin: poured pavers framed in steel, with light in the gravel joints

Our post on pavers with artificial turf follows the Cedar Creek deck from the plan to the finished yard. See our patios and our marbled concrete.

How Is a Modern Yard Lit?

From inside the structure. We build light under the rims of steel planters, along steel and deck edges, and under pergola beams. The source stays out of sight, and the light lands where it is used.

  • Lewis Mountain: LED strips trace the steel edges and the deck transitions
  • Pearson Ranch, Northwest Austin: the gravel joints between the concrete pavers are lit
  • Southeast Austin: a light rim runs around the outside of the steel beds, and the light washes down the black face
  • Austin: light sits under the concrete fire pit, which stands on hidden legs and appears to float

Red Agave is the only company in Austin that builds custom LED planters with custom light channels. Our systems run on 24V low voltage, in zones you control from a phone.

See our landscape lighting page and our post on landscape lighting installation.

Which Plants Suit a Modern Landscape?

Plants with a shape of their own. An agave holds its form all year and takes full sun, so one plant can carry a whole bed.

  • Southeast Austin: one variegated century agave in the triangle planter, with silver ponyfoot around it. Silver santolina, maiden grass, and artichoke agave grow in basalt gravel below.
  • Tarrytown: Agave weberi and artichoke agave in the steel planters, with blue spruce sedum over the edge
  • Austin: maiden grass in a steel bed along the fence, with sky pencil holly, soft leaf yucca, and whale’s tongue agave
  • Lewis Mountain: sedges, ferns, and muhly grass between the steel walls

The ground between the plants is part of the palette: basalt gravel, river rock, and Mexican beach pebbles. See our notes on artichoke agave, whale’s tongue agave, and Yucca rostrata.

Does a Modern Landscape Suit Austin?

Yes. Steel, concrete, and gravel need no water and no mowing. Under Austin Water’s Conservation Stage, in effect since September 2, 2025, automatic sprinklers run one day a week, so a yard that asks for little water fits the rules.

The plants are chosen the same way. Texas SmartScape lists whale’s tongue agave as cold hardy to 0°F, and NC State Extension lists artichoke agave as cold tolerant to -5°F when the soil is dry. Our post on freeze protection for plants has the dates and the limits. See our xeriscape work.

Where Does a Modern Yard Start?

On paper. Every Red Agave project starts with a free consultation, and the CAD plan is included. The owner draws every plan, and our own crew builds it.

For the Southeast Austin planter, the sketch was drawn over the front of the house, so it showed the steel where it would stand. See our design plans, our landscape design service, and our Mueller page for modern yards on small lots. For what a plan costs, see our landscape design cost guide.

Questions We Hear

What is modern landscaping?

A yard built from a short list of materials in straight lines. On our projects that is steel, large-format concrete, light with no visible source, and plants chosen for their shape.

What materials are used in modern landscaping in Austin?

Quarter-inch steel, poured concrete in large pads, gravel, river rock, and boulders. We cut and weld the steel on site, and we form, pour, and finish our own concrete.

What plants work in a modern Austin landscape?

Agaves, yuccas, and grasses. We have planted variegated century agave, Agave weberi, artichoke agave, whale’s tongue agave, maiden grass, and muhly grass on the projects in this post.

Is a modern landscape low maintenance?

The built parts are. Steel, concrete, and gravel need no water and no mowing, and the planting is chosen for your sun, your soil, and Austin’s watering limits.

Who designs and builds modern landscapes in Austin?

Red Agave Landscape. The owner draws every plan, and our own crew builds it. The consultation is free, and the CAD plan is included.
