Who Draws Red Agave’s Landscape Designs?

Every Red Agave landscape design is drawn by the owner, Jensen Mehrens, after a free on-site consultation in the Austin area. The drawings on this page are our own plans: pools, decks, fire courts, water features, paver terraces, and steel terraces on a slope. Each one sets the layout, the sizes, and the materials on paper, and then our own crew builds what we draw. The first three are shown next to the finished yards. Select any plan to enlarge it.

On Paper First

What Does a Red Agave Plan Show?

A plan is the yard decided before anything is ordered. These six parts make up a full landscape design.

Dimensioned PlanEvery bed, slab, wall, and path drawn to scale, with lengths and square footage on the drawing.
MaterialsSteel, concrete finishes, stone, and decking, chosen with samples in hand and approved before we order.
Plant PalettePlants chosen for your sun, your soil, and Austin’s watering limits.
Lighting Layout24V fixture locations and zones, planned together with the hardscape.
Grading and DrainageWhere the water goes after a storm, worked out before anything is built.
Sketches and 3DSketches over a photo of your house, and 3D renderings for complex projects.
Plan to Build

How Does a Design Become a Yard?

The team that draws the plan builds it. That is what design and build means at Red Agave, and you can see the results on our Signature Projects page.

  1. ConsultationFree and on site. We walk the yard with you and take measurements.
  2. Site StudySun, shade, drainage, soil, existing trees, views, and privacy.
  3. PlanDesign development takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on scope. The CAD plan is included.
  4. RevisionsWe revise until the plan fits the site and how you use it.
  5. ApprovalYou approve materials, plants, and every detail in writing.
  6. BuildOur own crew builds it: steel, concrete, stone, planting, and lighting.

Common Questions

Design Plan FAQ

Who draws Red Agave's landscape designs?

The owner, Jensen Mehrens. Every plan is owner-designed after a free on-site consultation, and our own crew builds what we draw.

What does a landscape design cost?

The consultation is free, and the CAD plan is included with your project.

What does a Red Agave plan show?

The layout, the dimensions, the square footage of each surface, and the materials. A full design also covers the plant palette, the lighting layout, and grading and drainage.

Can I see the design on my own house before it is built?

Yes. Where a plan is hard to picture, we sketch the design over a photo of your house. For complex projects, 3D renderings show materials and elevations before you sign off.

How long does a landscape design take?

Design development takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the scope of the project.

Are these real plans?

Yes. Every drawing on this page is our own work. Two of them, in Circle C and Southeast Austin, are shown next to the finished yards our crew built.
