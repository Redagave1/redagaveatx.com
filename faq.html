Design and Planning

More on our landscape design service, with real drawings on the design plans page.

Who designs my landscape?

The owner, Jensen Mehrens. Every plan is owner-designed after a free on-site consultation, and our own crew builds what we draw.

What is included in a landscape design plan?

A fully dimensioned 2D CAD plan, a plant palette, material selections, a lighting layout, grading and drainage, and an itemized quote. Complex projects also get 3D renderings.

How long does landscape design take?

Design development takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on project scope. Construction runs from about 4 weeks for a focused backyard to 8 to 16 weeks for a full property.

Is my tree protected in Austin?

Inside Austin city limits, Austin’s tree ordinance applies. Trees 19 inches in diameter or larger, measured 4.5 feet above the ground, are protected, and listed species 24 inches and up are heritage trees. Removing a protected tree takes a city permit, so we lay out every plan around the trunks first.

Steel

More on steel edging, steel retaining walls, and custom steel planters.

How thick is your steel edging?

One quarter inch. Thin edging bows under the soil behind it and bends under a mower. Quarter-inch plate stays straight.

Does steel edging rust through?

No. Mild steel forms a stable layer of rust that seals the steel under it. At a quarter inch thick, decades of surface rust leave far more steel than the edge needs.

Can steel edging be black?

Yes. Steel can be powder-coated in black, dark bronze, or charcoal. It can also be clear-coated to hold its dark gray color, or wax-sealed to hold a patina tone.

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall in Austin?

Austin Development Services lists a residential retaining wall as exempt from a building permit when it is not over 4 feet tall, measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, unless it supports a surcharge or sits in a flood hazard area. When a wall needs a permit, we handle it.

Patios and Concrete

More on patios, marbled concrete, and hardscaping.

What goes under a patio or walkway in Austin?

We excavate, compact the subgrade, and lay 6 to 8 inches of compacted crushed limestone, graded so water moves away from the house. Austin clay swells and shrinks, and this base keeps the surface flat.

What is marbled concrete?

A decorative finish created by swirling two or more colored concrete mixes during the pour. The color runs through the full depth, so it cannot wear off, and each pour creates a unique pattern.

Which patio surface stays coolest in the Austin sun?

Travertine. It stays cooler than concrete in direct sun, which is why it is used around pools.

Fire and Water

More on custom fire pits and water features.

Are fire pits legal in Austin, TX?

Yes. Both gas and wood-burning fire pits are allowed on residential properties in Austin. Gas fire pits have no seasonal restrictions. Wood-burning fire pits must be at least 15 feet from any structure and are subject to Travis County burn ban days during drought conditions.

Can you add a fire pit to my existing patio?

Yes. We add fire pits to existing concrete patios, flagstone patios, and deck areas. For a gas fire pit, we trench the gas line under or alongside the patio and connect to your existing gas meter. The fire pit sits on or is built into the existing surface.

Does a water feature use a lot of water?

No. A water feature recirculates its water through a pump, so the same water runs through it again and again. The only loss is evaporation, and an autofill valve replaces it.

Do water features bring mosquitoes?

Standing water breeds mosquitoes, and moving water does not. Every water feature we build has a pump that keeps the water moving.

Decks and Pergolas

More on custom decks and pergolas.

Why do you use steel framing instead of wood?

Mild steel frames do not warp, rot, split, or attract termites. In Austin’s heat, pressure-treated lumber can twist and cup within a few years. Steel stays flat and level for decades, and it allows longer spans without intermediate posts.

Do I need a permit for a deck in Austin?

Yes. The City of Austin requires a building permit for any attached deck and for freestanding decks over 200 square feet or 30 inches above grade. We handle all permitting as part of our design-build process, including structural engineering drawings, site plans, and inspections.

Do I need a permit for a pergola in Austin?

The City of Austin requires a building permit for pergolas attached to a home and for freestanding structures over 200 square feet. Our design-build process includes all permitting: engineering drawings, site plans, permit applications, and inspections.

Plants and Xeriscape

More on our xeriscape service and the native plants guide.

Will a xeriscape yard look dry and barren?

Not at all. A well-designed xeriscape is more colorful and textured than a conventional lawn. Native Texas plants like Flame Acanthus, Salvia Greggii, and Blackfoot Daisy bloom from spring through fall, and Mexican Feathergrass adds flowing movement.

What maintenance does a xeriscape yard need?

Very little. Native plants need supplemental water only during their first summer. After establishment, most need zero irrigation. Maintenance is limited to cutting back perennials once in late February, occasional weeding, and refreshing decomposed granite every 2 to 3 years. No mowing, no fertilizing, no fungicide.

Can my HOA stop me from installing xeriscape?

No. Texas Property Code §202.007 bars an HOA from prohibiting drought-resistant landscaping. The HOA can require plan review for aesthetic compatibility, but it cannot unreasonably deny or withhold approval.

How often can I water in Austin?

Austin Water customers are under the Conservation Stage, in effect since September 2, 2025. Automatic sprinklers run one day a week: Thursday for even addresses, Wednesday for odd, midnight to 10 a.m. or 7 p.m. to midnight. Drip and hose-end sprinklers get two days, and hand watering is allowed any time.

Lighting

More on our landscape lighting service.

What is FCOB lighting and how is it different?

FCOB (Flip Chip on Board) mounts tiny chips directly on a flexible substrate with a phosphor coating, producing a continuous, dot-free ribbon of light. Standard LED strip lights show individual dots of light separated by dark gaps.

What color temperature is best for landscape lighting?

2700K warm white is the most popular choice. It brings out stone, wood, and natural materials. 3000K works for modern homes that want a slightly crisper look. Avoid anything above 4000K at home: it looks cold and commercial.

Do I need an electrician for landscape lighting in Texas?

Not for the low-voltage lighting itself. Texas exempts landscapers installing low-voltage exterior lighting from the state electrician license. The power source is not exempt, so a new outlet or circuit needs a licensed electrician, and cities can add their own rules.
