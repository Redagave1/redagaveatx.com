The Ground You're Working With

Three Rules That Shape
a Bee Cave Yard

Red Agave designs and builds Bee Cave outdoor rooms for a DarkSky Community: steel and cedar pergolas for shade, warm light kept under the roofline and aimed down, and native screens that get by when the water utility cuts to once a week. Owner-designed, built by our own crew.
2023

A DarkSky Community

In April 2023, Bee Cave became the 7th DarkSky Community in Texas, under residential and commercial lighting rules that meet International Dark-Sky Association requirements. Outdoor light here is part of the code, not an afterthought.

1×

Water That Can Drop to Once a Week

Bee Cave’s watering rules come from the West Travis County Public Utility Agency, which follows LCRA drought plans. Stage 1 allows two days a week, 7 p.m. to 10 a.m., with no Sunday watering, and the agency drops to once a week when its plant runs above 85 percent capacity for three straight days. It did exactly that in August 2024, when heavy irrigation caused low pressure.

1987

A Young Hill Country City

Bee Cave incorporated in 1987 and is named for a large cave on the creek banks that swarmed with honeybees. Its population grew from 656 in 2000 to 3,925 in 2010 and about 6,515 by 2015.

What That Means for the Design

Put the Light Under a Roof,
Let the Natives Screen

Hill Country sun makes shade the first thing a Bee Cave yard needs, and a pergola gives it. We build them in mild steel, Western Red Cedar, or a steel and cedar hybrid, with motorized louvers as an option. See our pergolas service.

The pergola also helps with the lighting rules. Warm 2700K fixtures mounted under the beams and aimed down light the table and the kitchen, not the sky. Along paths, low shielded fixtures and 24V FCOB tucked under steel edges keep the glow at ground level. The Dripping Springs page covers the same dark-sky approach, and an outdoor kitchen under the same roof finishes the room.

Around the outdoor room go evergreen native screens and deep-rooted natives on drip, so a once-a-week schedule does not cost you the yard. Our xeriscape service covers the planting.

Questions From Bee Cave

Dark Skies, Water, and Shade

Is Bee Cave a dark-sky city?
Yes. In April 2023, Bee Cave became the 7th DarkSky Community in Texas, under residential and commercial lighting rules that meet International Dark-Sky Association requirements.
How does landscape lighting work under Bee Cave’s dark-sky rules?
Keep fixtures warm, shielded, and aimed down. We mount 2700K fixtures under pergola beams over tables and kitchens and keep path light low and shielded, so the glow stays on the ground.
How often can I water in Bee Cave?
Watering rules come from the West Travis County Public Utility Agency. Stage 1 allows two days a week, 7 p.m. to 10 a.m., with no Sunday watering, and the agency drops to once a week when its plant runs above 85 percent capacity for three straight days.
What kind of pergola works best in Bee Cave?
Mild steel gives clean lines and lasts; a steel and cedar hybrid adds the warmth of wood; motorized louvers let you choose full shade or full sun. A roof over the outdoor kitchen also keeps its lighting under the beams.
Who designs and builds the project?
Red Agave does both. Every plan is owner-designed, and our own crew builds it: pergolas, steel, concrete, planting, and lighting.
