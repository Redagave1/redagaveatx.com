The Ground You're Working With

Why a Round Rock Lawn
Depends on Which Side of Town

Red Agave builds Round Rock lawns and yards to match the ground under them: real sod where it gets used and watered, synthetic turf where grass struggles, and native beds for the rest, on a base built for clay east of the Balcones Fault Zone or limestone west of it. Owner-designed, built by our own crew.
Fault

A City Split by the Balcones Fault

The Balcones Fault Zone runs through Round Rock. West of it the ground is limestone; east of it is Blackland Prairie clay that swells when wet and cracks when dry. Two neighborhoods a few miles apart can need opposite base work. Even the city’s name comes from rock: an anvil-shaped limestone boulder in Brushy Creek, adopted as the name in 1854.

2 days

Two Watering Days, Before 10 or After 7

Round Rock’s water comes mainly from Lake Georgetown, with Lake Stillhouse Hollow water pumped into it and Edwards Aquifer and Lake Travis water as supplements. Under the city’s Year-Round Water Use Standards, sprinklers and drip run two days a week by address, before 10 a.m. or after 7 p.m.

25%

New Sod Needs a Variance

If more than 25 percent of a yard is new sod or new landscaping, the owner can apply to the city for a New Landscape Variance to get it established. Timing and paperwork are part of any big lawn renovation here.

What That Means for the Design

Sod Where You Use It,
Turf Where Grass Fails

Most Round Rock yards started as builder sod, and the question is which parts earn their water. Keep real grass where kids and dogs use it and the sun is strong: Bermuda wants 6 or more hours, Zoysia 4 or more. Put synthetic turf in the shade, the dog run, and the side yard where grass never takes. Our turf installation service covers both, and the natural vs. artificial turf comparison helps you decide.

The base follows the fault line. Sod goes down on amended, graded soil; synthetic turf goes on drainage fabric and compacted decomposed granite, graded so water leaves the yard. On east-side clay that grading is what keeps the yard from holding water; on west-side limestone the work is getting enough soil depth over rock for roots. The alkaline soil guide explains what the limestone does to plants.

Whatever comes out of the lawn becomes native beds with steel edging, on drip. For a big conversion, plan around the New Landscape Variance so the new planting gets established. See our xeriscape service.

Planting

Natives for Clay
and for Rock

On the clay side, natives that take heavy alkaline soil: Texas sage, autumn sage, flame acanthus, and Gulf muhly. On the limestone side, agaves and yuccas in fast-draining beds, with blackfoot daisy at the edges. Our cold-hardy agave guide lists agaves that take a freeze.

Everything goes on drip so it lives on two watering days. Our native plants guide and summer survivors list have more.

Seep Muhly UNDAUNTED®

Muhlenbergia reverchonii ‘PUND01S’

The Balcones Fault Zone splits Round Rock: limestone to the west, Blackland clay to the east. Seep muhly grows wild on both, so the same grass works on either side of town. It stays compact enough for a front bed and turns ruby in fall.

Questions From Round Rock

Lawn, Water, and Rock

How often can I water in Round Rock?
Under Round Rock’s Year-Round Water Use Standards, sprinklers and drip irrigation run two days a week by address, before 10 a.m. or after 7 p.m.
Can I water new sod more often in Round Rock?
If more than 25 percent of a yard is new sod or new landscaping, the owner can apply to the city for a New Landscape Variance.
Is Round Rock soil clay or limestone?
Both. The Balcones Fault Zone runs through Round Rock, with limestone to the west and Blackland Prairie clay to the east, so base work and plant choices change across town.
Should I choose sod or artificial turf?
Real sod suits sunny spots that get used. Synthetic turf suits shade, dog runs, and side yards where grass never takes. Quality turf with UV-stabilized fiber lasts 15 to 25 years in Texas.
Where does Round Rock’s water come from?
Mainly Lake Georgetown, with Lake Stillhouse Hollow water pumped into it and Edwards Aquifer and Lake Travis water as supplements.
