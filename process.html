How Does a Red Agave Project Work?

A Red Agave Landscape project runs in six steps: a free on-site consultation, a site study, a CAD plan drawn by the owner, revisions and written approval, the build by our own crew, and a final walkthrough. The CAD plan is included with your project. One team carries the work from the first site visit to the final walkthrough, with one point of contact. Red Agave has designed and built Austin landscapes since 2009. Call or text (512) 695-7566.
Consult · Design · Build · Deliver

Six Steps, One Team

  1. 01

    Free Consultation

    The consultation is free and on site. We meet you at your property, walk the yard with you, and take measurements, with no charge and no obligation. Consultations run Tuesday through Thursday, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., or by special arrangement, and we confirm every request within 24 hours. Nothing is required beforehand. Photos of your yard or a survey, uploaded with your booking, help us arrive prepared.

  2. 02

    Site Study

    Design starts at your property, not in an office. We study the grade, trace the shade lines, and photograph the sightlines from inside the house. Then we look at what the site demands: sun, drainage, soil, existing trees, views, and privacy. Where rock or caliche matters, we dig test holes.

  3. 03

    The Plan

    The owner, Jensen Mehrens, draws every plan. You receive a fully dimensioned 2D CAD plan, with every bed, slab, wall, and path drawn to scale. A full landscape design also sets the plant palette, the materials, the lighting layout, and the grading and drainage. Where a plan is hard to picture, we sketch the design over a photo of your house, and complex projects get 3D renderings. Design development takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on scope.

  4. 04

    Revisions and Approval

    Revisions are included. We revise until the plan fits the site and how you use it. We bring material and color samples, and you approve materials, plants, and every detail in writing, with an itemized quote, before construction starts.

  5. 05

    The Build

    Our own crew builds what we draw: steel, concrete, stone, planting, and lighting. Site preparation comes first, with demolition, grading, drainage, and utility rough-ins. Hardscape is the permanent structure of a yard, so it goes in before planting and lighting. We provide a detailed schedule before breaking ground, and you get daily progress photos and a weekly walkthrough with your project lead. Gas line connections go to a licensed plumber, and major electrical panel work goes to a licensed electrician. When a project needs a permit, we handle it.

  6. 06

    Final Walkthrough

    Before a project is complete, we walk the yard with you and a formal punch list. Each item is documented and signed off by you and your project lead. You receive a care packet covering irrigation schedules, seasonal planting guidance, and material maintenance for every surface we installed. All steel carries a 10-year warranty, and a continuing maintenance program is available for clients who want it.

Why One Team Designs and Builds

Most landscaping companies either design or build. Red Agave does both, and that changes three things for you:

  • One point of contact. One team carries the project from the first site visit to the final walkthrough
  • A buildable plan. The plan is drawn by the people who cut the steel and set the boulders
  • Materials chosen by the crew that builds. We select and source every material, and you approve the selections before we order

Our design-build service page compares one team with hiring a designer and a contractor separately.

All at Once or in Phases

We draw the whole property at once, even if you build it in phases. Infrastructure like drainage, electrical, and gas is planned first, so later phases never tear up finished work. Building all at once is more efficient: you mobilize equipment once and excavate once.

Process Questions

Is the consultation free?

Yes. We meet you at your property, walk the space together, and take measurements with no charge and no obligation. Consultations run Tuesday through Thursday, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., or by special arrangement.

Is the CAD plan included?

Yes. The on-site consultation is free, and a custom CAD plan is included with your project.

Who designs my project?

The owner, Jensen Mehrens. Every plan is owner-designed, and our own crew builds what we draw.

How long does the design take?

Design development takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the scope of the project.

How long does construction take?

Construction runs from about 4 weeks for a focused backyard to 8 to 16 weeks for a full property. We provide a detailed schedule before breaking ground.

Will I see progress while my project is built?

Yes. You receive daily progress photos and a weekly walkthrough with your project lead, and the project closes with a formal punch list.

Can I build in phases?

Yes. We draw the whole property at once, even if you build it in phases. Drainage, electrical, and gas are planned first, so later phases never tear up finished work.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. When a project needs a permit, we handle it.
