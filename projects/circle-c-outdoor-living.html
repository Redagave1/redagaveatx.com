The Challenge · Circle C

Dated bones. New skin.

Built Over · Not Demolished

A Circle C backyard that had surrendered to time — crumbling flagstone patio with moss creeping through every joint, a sun-bleached cedar pergola leaning under its own weight, and bare caliche dirt where the lawn gave up two summers ago. The homeowners wanted a complete outdoor living transformation without the demolition timeline.

The brief: build over the existing bones, not through them. Lay a continuous composite deck plane directly over the flagstone. Reframe the sagging cedar pergola with structural mild steel and CNC-cut shadow panels. Integrate fire, seating, and lighting into the architecture — no aftermarket accessories, no bolt-on fixtures.

Where most contractors would quote a two-week demolition phase before any building could begin, Red Agave turned the existing substrate into the foundation. Every element was custom-fabricated and installed by the same crew — 14 days, zero subcontractors, zero change orders.

Engineering Solution

Built over. Not demolished.

Structural · 01 Deck + Pergola System Sea Salt Composite · Mild Steel Reframe 480 sq ft of Dekorators "Sea Salt" composite decking laid in one continuous plane directly over the existing flagstone substrate. The original cedar pergola was stripped to its posts and reframed with structural mild steel. Six CNC-cut 1/4-inch mild steel triangle panels mounted across the top throw geometric shadow patterns that shift with the sun.

System Details

Steel Pergola Reframe

  • —Original cedar stripped to posts, reframed with structural mild steel
  • —6 CNC-cut steel triangle shadow panels, 1/4-inch mild steel
  • —Powder-coated matte black finish for UV and corrosion resistance
  • —Geometric shadow patterns that shift throughout the day

Composite Deck System

  • —480 sq ft Dekorators "Sea Salt" composite decking
  • —Laid over existing flagstone — zero demolition required
  • —2 oversized composite steps (8 ft wide x 18 in deep)
  • —Steel-framed outdoor seating wall (12 lf)

Fire + Seating Integration

  • —Hand-set limestone boulder fire pit cast into step landing
  • —4 cantilevered steel planters with concealed COB uplights
  • —12 lf steel-framed seating wall integrated into deck edge
  • —Fire pit and planters built as part of structure, not aftermarket add-ons

FCOB Perimeter Lighting

  • —24V FCOB perimeter lighting under deck coping (120 lf)
  • —ESP32 micro-controller running WLED firmware
  • —2700K warm-white ambient via app-based control
  • —Zero visible hardware — all runs concealed in milled channels
