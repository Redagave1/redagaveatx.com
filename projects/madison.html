Design Intent · Crestview, Austin

Bare dirt by the pool. Now turf, steel, and fire.

Turf · Steel · Boulder · Concrete

Red Agave Landscape designed and built this backyard in Crestview, Austin, in 2024: a slightly terraced yard in artificial turf, with two lit tapered steel planters, a floating boulder fire pit, a floating bench, and marbled concrete formed in steel. It started as a bare piece of dirt next to the pool. The ground had a slight slope, so we terraced it slightly, stepping down to the seating area. The turf is Ultra Real, made by American Synthetic Turf.

There are two tapered steel planters, each shaped like a V. Each one sits on two turf steps. Each has a light in its upper rim, and a cutout in the steel with a second low voltage light. The COB lights are BTF, and the lighting in this yard is low voltage. A weeping redbud grows in each planter.

The fire pit is a black diamond boulder. The owner of Red Agave drilled a hole in the bottom of the boulder and mounted it on an oil field pipe. Mirrors are mounted under the boulder, so it looks like it is floating. The gas line goes through the pipe. A hole was drilled through the boulder for the gas line. The top was carved out and filled with fire glass that looks like obsidian. A second boulder floats above the turf as a bench, with mirrors mounted under it as well.

The concrete was finished smooth with a marbling effect. It is formed with 1/4-inch by 4-inch steel, and the steel remains in place. The planting is weeping redbud, native sedge, variegated cast iron plant, gayfeather, Japanese yews, Japanese maple, cherry laurel, ferns, and philodendron. The deck is stained with Ready Seal.

Questions about this build

What was this backyard before Red Agave built it?
A bare piece of dirt next to the pool. The ground had a slight slope, so Red Agave terraced it slightly, stepping down to the seating area. The turf is Ultra Real, made by American Synthetic Turf. The pool was already there.
What are the V-shaped steel planters, and how are they lit?
They are tapered steel planters, and there are two of them. Each one has a light in its upper rim and a cutout with a second low voltage light. The COB lights are BTF. Each planter sits on two turf steps.
How does the boulder fire pit float?
The fire pit is a black diamond boulder. The owner of Red Agave drilled a hole in the bottom of it and mounted it on an oil field pipe. Mirrors are mounted under the boulder, at the base, so the boulder looks like it is floating. The gas line goes through the pipe and through a hole drilled through the boulder. The top is carved out and filled with fire glass that looks like obsidian. The floating bench has mirrors under it too.
How was the concrete formed?
With 1/4-inch by 4-inch steel, and the steel remains in place. The concrete was finished smooth with a marbling effect.
What is planted in this yard?
Weeping redbud, native sedge, variegated cast iron plant, gayfeather, Japanese yews, Japanese maple, cherry laurel, ferns, and philodendron.
Who designed and built it?
Red Agave Landscape designed it, and it was built by our own crew in 2024. Every project starts with a free consultation, and the CAD plan is included.
