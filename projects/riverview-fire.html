The Challenge · East Austin

Crumbling wall. No gathering space.

Statement Fire Feature · Zero Off-the-Shelf

The backyard had a crumbling retaining wall and no usable gathering space. What should have been the heart of the property was an afterthought — a neglected slope of broken block and bare dirt that made the entire outdoor area feel disconnected from the home.

The homeowner wanted a statement fire feature — not a Home Depot ring, not a tile-clad box from a big-box catalog. Something welded, structural, singular. A piece that looked like it belonged in a gallery courtyard, not a suburban patio. Red Agave fabricated a floating welded steel fire pit from 1/4-inch mild steel plate, suspended on concealed steel legs over a gravel bed with COB uplighting beneath.

The old retaining wall was torn out and rebuilt with black cantera stone set on an angle-steel ledge with integrated COB wash lighting at the base. Concrete was poured to look like marble — an artisan marbled finish framed in 1/4-inch steel edging. Native Texas plantings complete the space. The entire build executed 100% in-house — zero subcontracted trades.

Engineering Solution

Welded steel. Not assembled.

Fire Feature · 01 Floating Fire Pit 1/4" Mild Steel · Welded Box 1/4-inch mild steel plate fabricated into a welded box construction. 48-inch diameter fire bowl with a 24-inch stainless steel burner ring plumbed to natural gas. Suspended on concealed steel legs over a decomposed granite bed. Four 2700K COB uplights (CRI 90+) mounted beneath the fire pit, controlled via ESP32 WLED.
