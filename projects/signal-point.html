The Project · East Austin

A poured concrete fire pit, a steel court, and jasmine on the trellis.

Poured Concrete · Steel · Gravel · Jasmine

Red Agave Landscape designed and built this backyard fire court on Signal Point in East Austin in 2018. The fire pit is concrete, custom formed, framed and poured for this yard. It sits on a raised court of dark gravel framed in steel, and a steel trellis wraps around the seating and carries jasmine.

The lines of the form boards show on the faces of the fire pit. The steel frame holds the gravel above the lawn, and a second, lower steel frame makes the step up from the grass. The trellis is steel posts and rails with wire grid panels. It runs along two sides of the court, and the jasmine climbs the panels behind the seating.
