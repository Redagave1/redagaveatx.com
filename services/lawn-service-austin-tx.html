Mowing Height

How High Should Austin Grass Be Cut?

Higher than most lawns are cut. Taller grass shades the soil, slows evaporation, and keeps the roots 10 to 15 degrees cooler than scalped turf.

GrassSummer HeightSummer Mowing
St. Augustine3.5 to 4 inchesEvery 7 to 10 days
Bermuda1.5 to 2 inchesEvery 5 to 7 days
Zoysia2.5 to 3 inchesEvery 7 to 10 days
Buffalo grass3 to 4 inchesEvery 10 to 14 days

One rule covers every grass: never remove more than one third of the blade in a single mowing. If St. Augustine has reached 6 inches and the target is 4, it is cut to 4.5 first and mowed again three or four days later. Scalping exposes the soil to direct sun.

Thin Lawns

Why Does a Lawn Thin Out?

For a reason you can name. Grass does not thin at random. In Central Texas it recedes from five stresses.

StressWhat HappensWhat Helps
SlopeRain runs off and takes topsoil with itCompost on the slope, and watering in short repeated cycles
Tree rootsRoots take the water and the top 6 to 8 inches of soilMulch, decomposed granite, or shade groundcover inside the drip line
ShadeGrass is a sun plant. St. Augustine and Zoysia need 4 to 6 hours of sun to stay dense.Selective thinning of the canopy, or shade plants
Low nutrientsSoil here is alkaline, pH 7.5 to 8.5, and low in organic matterCompost and natural fertilizer
Little soil lifeCompacted soil sheds water and crusts overCompost first, then aeration

The fix starts with the soil. A half-inch layer of compost raked into the turf, once in spring and once in fall, rebuilds the organic layer. A natural fertilizer such as MicroLife 6-2-4 feeds the soil as well as the grass.

Aeration comes after that. Core aeration pulls plugs of soil to open channels for air, water, and roots. In soil with no organic matter the holes collapse within days, so compost goes down first. Water

How Is a Lawn Watered Under Austin's Rules?

On one sprinkler day a week. Austin Water's Conservation Stage has been in effect since September 2, 2025.

WateringAustin Water Rule
Automatic sprinklersOne day a week: Thursday for even addresses, Wednesday for odd
HoursMidnight to 10 a.m., or 7 p.m. to midnight
Hose-end sprinklers and dripTwo days a week
Hand wateringAny time

One day is enough when the water soaks in. On clay, each zone is run for 10 to 12 minutes, left for 30 to 60 minutes to absorb, and run again. A screwdriver pushed into the soil after watering should slide in to 6 inches.

Edges

What Keeps the Edge of a Lawn Clean?

Steel. An edge between the lawn and the bed keeps the grass out of the gravel and the mulch out of the grass.

Most edging sold for landscapes is plastic or thin steel, and a mower bends it. We weld quarter-inch steel plate into one continuous band. It is thick enough to be driven over by a mower without showing it. Bermuda in particular spreads into beds that have no steel edge.

Mulch

Why Mulch the Beds Around a Lawn?

Because mulch does four jobs at once. Three to four inches of hardwood mulch in the beds:

  • Cools the soil: by 10 to 20 degrees compared with open soil
  • Holds water: it cuts evaporation by 50 to 70 percent
  • Stops weeds: seeds under it do not germinate
  • Feeds the soil: it breaks down and improves the clay over time

Mulch is kept 3 to 4 inches back from tree trunks and plant stems. When it has broken down below 2 inches, it is topped up before summer.

Less Lawn

When Is Grass the Wrong Answer?

Where it will not grow. Under a dense live oak, on a steep slope, or in a narrow strip a mower cannot reach, the answer is a different surface.

  • Under trees: mulch, decomposed granite, or shade groundcover inside the drip line
  • Hot, dry ground: native beds, gravel, and boulders. See our xeriscape page.
  • Shade, dog runs, and side yards: artificial turf. See our comparison of artificial turf and real grass.

Not every yard needs to lose its lawn. Kids need a place to run, and dogs need a place to play. We keep lawn where it gets used and replace it where it struggles.
