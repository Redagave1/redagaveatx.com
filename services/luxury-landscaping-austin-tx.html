What Makes a High-End Landscaper in Austin?

Red Agave Landscape is a high-end design-build landscaper in Austin, TX, founded in 2009, with 750+ projects completed. Every project is owner-designed and built by our own crew in 1/4-inch mild steel, 4,000 PSI concrete with fiber mesh and rebar, natural stone, and 24V FCOB lighting on WLED controllers. Fully insured, with a 10-year warranty on all steel. Call or text 512-695-7566.

High-end is not a price. It is what happens under the surface and in the details: how deep the base goes, how thick the steel is, where the water runs, and whether the light hides its source. Those decisions are what keep a yard looking right years after the crew leaves.

We design every project and build it with our own crew, so one standard runs from the first drawing to the last fixture. Steel, concrete, hardscape, planting, and lighting are all built by our crew. It starts with a landscape design drawn for your property.

How to Choose

How to Choose a High-End Landscaper in Austin

01
Who Builds It
Ask whether the crew on your site works for the company you hired, or for someone they hired.
02
The Steel
Ask the gauge. Walls and edging that hold a clean line are 1/4-inch plate, welded, not thin edging staked in place.
03
The Concrete
Ask the PSI, the reinforcement, and what goes under the slab. Austin clay punishes a thin base.
04
The Water
Ask to see drainage on the plan. If water is not designed, it ends up against the house.
05
The Light
Ask what system runs the lighting and whether it needs a subscription or a proprietary hub.
06
The Proof
Ask for proof of insurance, the warranty in writing, and finished projects you can see.

More questions to ask are in our guide to choosing a landscaper in Austin.
